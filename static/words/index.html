About the Reader

What is this?

A lightning-fast, highly optimized offline reader for exploring Osho's discourses. It allows you to search across thousands of texts instantly.

Do I need internet?

Only for the very first visit! Once the library initializes, the entire database is stored securely on your device. You can turn on airplane mode and it will still work perfectly.

How does searching work?

Type any keyword in the search box. The app will scan all available discourses and rank the results based on how often your word appears. You can also click "Explore" to browse full books.

Can I export my research?

Yes. When reading, tap the menu (⋮) in the top right. You can download the matches from just the current page you are reading, or export every single match across the entire library.
